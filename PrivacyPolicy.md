# **GrammatikArtikel**
## Privacy Policy
**Developer:** The Chauncy Files
**Last updated:** 2026-03-21

---

## 1. What Data We Collect
To provide a persistent learning experience and track your progress through the 1,000+ German nouns in our database, **GrammatikArtikel** collects limited performance data.

* **User Statistics & Progress:** We store your success rates with noun articles, mastery levels, and study session timestamps to provide accuracy tracking and personalized learning stats.
* **Unique Identifiers:** We use an anonymous, Firebase-generated installation ID to distinguish your progress and settings from other users. This ID is not linked to your name, email address, or any personally identifying information.

**No personal identity data (such as your name or email) is collected, and no user data will ever be sold.**

## 2. Data Storage & Transmission
* **Local Storage:** Core app settings and progress data are stored in your device's local SQLite database and SharedPreferences. This data is fully accessible offline.
* **Cloud Storage:** Progress and stats data are synchronized to **Cloud Firestore** (a service provided by Google) to enable backup and restore across devices. All data transmitted off-device is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

## 3. Third-Party Services
This app uses **Firebase** (including Cloud Firestore) for data storage and sync. Google may process your data according to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not use third-party advertising, trackers, or data brokers.

## 4. Children's Privacy
This app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect any personal information as defined by COPPA. Data stored in Firebase is strictly limited to anonymous learning-progress metrics.

## 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion
You have full control over the data generated by your use of the app:

* **In-App Self-Service Delete:** Open **Settings → Delete Player**, type your player name to confirm, and tap "Schedule Delete." A 72-hour cancellation window begins immediately. If not cancelled, all local progress and cloud data are permanently deleted when the window expires (on the next app launch after the 72 hours). This process is fully automatic — no email required.
* **In-App Email Request:** Open **Settings → Bug / Feedback / Delete Data**, select the "Delete Data" category, and submit. Your Firebase User ID is included automatically. We will fulfill deletion requests within 30 days.
* **Local Deletion:** Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
* **Cloud Deletion:** Cloud Firestore data is deleted automatically by the in-app self-service flow, or within 30 days of a manual email request.

For the full data deletion policy, visit: [sites.google.com/view/grammatikartikel/deletion](https://sites.google.com/view/grammatikartikel/deletion)

## 6. Changes to This Policy
If our data practices change, the updated version will be included in app updates. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
