# **GrammatikArtikel**
## Privacy Policy
**Developer:** The Chauncy Files  
**Last updated:** 2026-03-11

---

## 1. What Data We Collect
To provide a persistent learning experience and track your progress through the 1,000+ German nouns in our database, **GrammatikArtikel** collects limited performance and technical data.

* **User Statistics & Progress:** We store your success rates with noun articles and study session timestamps to provide accuracy tracking and personalized learning stats.
* **Unique Identifiers:** We use an anonymous, Firebase-generated installation ID to distinguish your progress and settings from other users. 
* **Technical Metadata:** Basic device information (OS version, device model) is processed to ensure the 1,200+ WebP assets render correctly on your hardware.

**I promise that no personal identity data (such as your name or email) is collected, and no user data will ever be sold.**

## 2. Data Storage & Transmission
* **Local Storage:** Core app settings and immediate progress are stored in your device's local SQLite database.
* **Cloud Storage:** Synchronized stats and progress data are transmitted to and stored securely in the **Firebase Realtime Database** (a service provided by Google).
* **Encryption:** All data transmitted off-device to Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

## 3. Third-Party Services
This app uses **Firebase** for data management and storage. Google may process your data according to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not use third-party advertising, trackers, or data brokers.

## 4. Children's Privacy
This app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect any personal information as defined by COPPA. Data stored in Firebase is strictly limited to anonymous app-performance metrics.

## 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion
You have total control over the data generated by your use of the app:
* **Local Deletion:** Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
* **Server Deletion:** If you wish to have your progress stats deleted from our Firebase console, please contact us at the email below. We will fulfill all deletion requests within 30 days.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
If our data practices change, the updated version will be included in app updates. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
